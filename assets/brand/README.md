# Brand asset provenance

`whatsapp-glyph-white.svg` is the unmodified Digital_Glyph_White_RGB_2026.svg from Meta’s official WhatsApp Brand Resource Center logo pack, retrieved 13 November 2026.

Source: https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/whatsapp/whatsapp-brand/

The glyph retains its original geometry and white fill. The contact button uses the standard WhatsApp green; the attention halo is outside the asset. The site does not claim WhatsApp endorsement or partnership.
